## Research: Business of AI DCs w/ TechButMakeItReal | YouTube Series
This video breaks down the complex economic ecosystem behind AI subscriptions, revealing that your monthly fee is distributed across a multi-layered infrastructure stack rather than primarily benefiting the AI companies themselves. It traces the journey of a single query—from edge servers and API gateways to the critical, compute-heavy inference phase where most costs are incurred. The narrative highlights the dominant role of hyperscalers and data center landlords, who generate stable, durable revenue through long-term power and infrastructure contracts, effectively functioning as the "shovel sellers" of the modern AI gold rush. Ultimately, the video argues that the true winners in the AI boom are the foundational providers of GPU capacity, electricity, and physical data center real estate, whose profitability is secured regardless of which specific AI model or application a user chooses.

Understanding how your subscription expenses are allocated across services helps you identify unnecessary spending, make informed budgeting decisions, and get the most value from the services you use.
