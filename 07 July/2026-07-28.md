## Networking Today | The Hedge podcast: Out of Band Management Networks

An out of band network is a dedicated autonomous system that is different from your primary network. It is physically segregated. 

Out of band cost use is determined on a case-by-case basis.

OOB network design and considerations.

"Modern" use-case; zero-trust approach to management, staff access, etc.

## Benefits:
|Benefit|Description|
|---|---:|
|Continuous Access | Ensures uninterrupted access without needing physical access|
|Enhanced Security Layer| Reduces impact surface |
|Improved Recovery| Enables faster recovery through access and remote restoration |
|Cost Efficiency| Can minimize downtime and associated recovery costs via immediate access |
