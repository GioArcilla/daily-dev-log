## Worked On
- Troubleshooting methodologies

## Reviewed | High Level
1) Define the problem: systems affected, number of users/machines affected, level of severity and escalation tiers
2) Gather information: analyze monitoring metrics, check logs, relay timestamps, monitor error messages
3) Establish a theory: based on information gathered, create a most likely probable cause
4) Test and Isolate: depending on severity of the issue, isolate and test systems, vice versa, or both
5) Implement, Monitor and Verify: apply fix to an environment and closely track functionality before attempting to push to production
6) Document: record any and all findings to create actionable steps for future reference and to potentially prevent the issue from resurfacing
