## Zero Trust Approach:
|Benefit|Description| 
|---|---:|
|Never Trust, Always Verify| Every used, device, and workload must be authenticated and authorized|
|Verify Explicitly| Decisions use identity, device health, location, behavior, and other metric signals|
|Least-Privilege Access| Access is limited to what is strictly necessary|
|Assume Breach| Systems are designed with the expectation that attackers may or may not already be inside|
|Continuous Monitoring| Device posture, sessions, and behavior are constantly evaluated|
