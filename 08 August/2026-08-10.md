# August 10, 2026

On board Delta Flight 591 today, 10th day of August, evil twin network was created to intercept passenger data. This was a flight carrying passengers from DEF CON 34 conference.

## Cyberthreats:

|Threat| Description|
|---|---:|
|Email phishing| Fraudulent emails that appear legitimate|
|Vishing| Voice calls pretending to be trusted sources to extract sensitive information|
|Smishing| Fraudulent text messages with malicious links or requests|
|Pharming| Users being redirected to fakes sites by device or DNS manipulation|
|Evil Twin| Creating fake public networks to intercept data|
