## Preventive Controls:

|Function | Description|
|---|---:|
|Access Control| User authentication, RBAC, and least privilege principles to restrict unauthorized access|
|Firewalls/IPS| Monitor and filter network traffic to block malicious activity|
|Encryption| Protects data at rest and in transit, ensuring unauthorized access does not compromise sensitive information|
|Policies/Procedures| Guidelines for password management, data handling, and acceptable use to enforce secure behavior|
|MFA| Mechanism for verifying user identify using more than one source of information|
