## Importance of Hardware Testing

|Reason| Description|
|---|---:|
|Validation| Ensures that physical components are safe, reliable, functional, and durable|
|Quality Assurance| Ensures that components meet design specifications and compliance|
|Cost Savings| Early detection of issues reduces costly rework and recalls|
|Customer Satisfaction| Improves user experience, builds trust, and increases satisfaction|
