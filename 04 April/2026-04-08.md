## Reviewed: Suricata

My goal in reviewing the capabilities of Suricata is to be able to understand the essentials of IDS/IPS systems. Today I went on a bit of a in-depth research on remote connection, Suricata initial configuration and its defaults, as well as quickly finding out about windows RDP not supporting a lot of essentials for remote connection. 

Battling my awesome power of getting easily distracted with other softwares and capabilities, I've came to the conclusion of the key functions of IDS/IPS and four primary considerations:

**Intrusion Detection Systems** are best used for monitoring traffic, for ensuring regulatory compliance, and for identifying suspicious activity without dirupting flow. 

**Intrusion Prevention Systems** are active, real-time protection against known threats, malware, and exploits. 

1) **Function**
   - IDS: detects and notifies
   - IPS: detects and execute
3) **Deployment**
   - IDS: out-of-band (ie analyzing a copy of traffic)
   - IPS: inline (traffic passes through)
5) **Response Time**
   - IDS: alerts, allowing for scheduled analysis
   - IPS: provides immediate, automated defense
7) **Impact**
   - IDS: minimal impact on network performance
   - IPS: when improperly configured, can introduce latency or false positives
