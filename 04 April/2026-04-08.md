## Reviewed: Suricata

My goal in reviewing the capabilities of Suricata is to be able to understand the essentials of IDS/IPS systems. Today I went on a bit of a in-depth research on remote connection, Suricata initial configuration and its defaults, as well as quickly finding out about windows RDP not supporting a lot of essentials for remote connection. 

Battling my awesome power of getting easily distracted with other softwares and capabilities, I've came to the conclusion of the key functions of IDS/IPS and four primary considerations:

**Intrusion <ins>Detection</ins> Systems** are best used for monitoring traffic, for ensuring regulatory compliance, and for identifying suspicious activity without dirupting flow. 

**Intrusion <ins>Prevention</ins> Systems** are active, real-time protection against known threats, malware, and exploits. 

||**IDS**|**IPS**|
|---|:---:|:---:|
|**Function**|detects and notifies|detects and executes|
|**Deployment**|out-band (ie analyzing a copy of traffic|inline (traffic passes through)|
|**Response Time**|alerts,allowing for scheduled analysis|provides immediate, automated defense|
|**Impact**|minimal impact on network performance|when improperly configured, can introduce latency or false positive|
